# Public Findings: Valko / Ao Yin Controversy, 2026-06-22 to 2026-06-30

## Scope

This is a privacy-preserving public summary of a larger source-reconstruction project examining the Valko / Ao Yin implementation controversy in *Love and Deepspace*.

The working reconstruction contains 217 records. During final review, 202 URLs were re-checked directly through preserved Sina pages, while 15 records required separate review through official Weibo material, preserved related-post views, profile-history evidence, or other public traces.

This is not a census of Weibo and is not used to estimate majority opinion.

In public outputs, existing male leads are masked as **LI-A to LI-E**. Character-specific hashtags that would reveal those identities are also generalized. Fan usernames, user IDs, post IDs, direct fan-post links, and verbatim quotations are not published.

## Main findings

### 1. The opposition space cannot be explained by one shared motive

Before and during the sixth-LI controversy, different existing-character communities were already discussing different unresolved issues: character-specific story and setting consistency, production-resource allocation, card and main-story scheduling, mixed-banner dilution, spending strategy, OOC concerns, and operator distrust.

These issues temporarily overlapped around the sixth-LI controversy, but they were not identical.

### 2. The A-0731 escalation had distinguishable stages

The reconstructed June 29 sequence shows separate stages including early problem identification, explicit mention of A-0731, historical / national-trauma framing, external-media or public-institution appeals, integration with women's-safety concerns, relay and amplification, and internal criticism.

These roles were not necessarily performed by the same accounts.

### 3. Direct connection to the named anti-company community appears later than the early A-0731 formation in the recovered set

Within the recovered material, explicit A-0731 identification, historical framing, and external mobilization were visible before a directly verified participant in the pre-existing anti-company community connected that space to A-0731.

This does not prove that no earlier deleted or unrecovered connection existed. It does mean the present source set does not support treating that community as the demonstrated origin of the A-0731 frame.

### 4. Goals diverged within minutes after cancellation

After the June 30 cancellation announcement, the overlapping opposition space quickly separated into different directions: return to existing-character-specific demands, continued anti-company action, refusal to return, criticism of the cancellation, explicit calls for Valko to be restored, and claims of partial or first-stage success.

## Interpretation

The reconstruction is more consistent with **temporary convergence of multiple pre-existing grievances, followed by rapid frame expansion and then renewed divergence after cancellation**, than with a single stable bloc acting from one motive and one source.

This does not establish the direct cause of the company's cancellation decision, nor does it prove the absence of any coordination. Those are separate causal questions that would require additional evidence.
