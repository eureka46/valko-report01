What this page shows

This is not the full 217-record working dataset. It is a representative public subset of 29 anonymized entries selected to show what the underlying research actually looked like: which concerns appeared before the announcement, how positions diverged across character communities, how the A-0731 frame developed during June 29, and how the temporary coalition fragmented immediately after cancellation.

Fan identities are intentionally removed. Time is generalized into broad windows for fan posts when minute-level precision would increase re-identification risk. Official company statements remain identifiable because they are institutional public records.
