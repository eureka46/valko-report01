Selected findings

Opposition was not one unified bloc

Different character communities already carried distinct story, setting, production, card, monetization and promotional grievances. Their interests temporarily overlapped around the sixth-LI issue without becoming identical.

Existing-character fandom and Valko support could coexist

The reconstructed set contains counterexamples showing that primary support for an existing LI did not automatically imply opposition to Valko.

A-0731 spread through distributed roles

Identification, reframing, relay, amplification, external mobilization and internal criticism were not necessarily performed by the same people.

Cancellation produced immediate divergence

Within minutes, the overlapping coalition separated into character-specific demands, continued anti-company action, cancellation criticism, restoration calls and partial-success narratives.

Collection, rules & ethics

Search-based reconstruction

Candidate material was discovered through public-web search and then checked at source level. This dataset was not acquired through a researcher-operated high-frequency crawler directly against Weibo.

Platform-rule review

Weibo's Community Convention Article 54 restricts unauthorized automated or abnormally high-frequency behavior including data extraction. This project does not claim blanket platform approval or a general right to scrape.

Privacy by design

Public outputs remove fan identifiers, direct fan-post links, post IDs and verbatim quotations, and generalize some timestamps to reduce re-identification risk.

The detailed methodology page also documents the PRC Personal Information Protection Law Article 27 as a privacy consideration and uses Association of Internet Researchers ethics guidance as a framework for contextual privacy and harm reduction. Read Methods & Ethics →

Research principles

Findings are not interpretation

What a post demonstrably says is kept separate from whether its interpretation is true or whether it caused later events.

Traceable uncertainty

Image-dependent, deleted-original, related-panel-only and otherwise weaker records are treated differently from directly verified public pages.

No prevalence claims from this set

The 217-record reconstruction is not a census or probability sample and is not used to estimate majority opinion within a fandom.
