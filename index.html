What this project does

Source reconstruction

Reconstructs event sequences from accessible public traces while distinguishing directly verified material from weaker evidence.

Fact-checking

Separates findings, hypotheses, questions, and later interpretation instead of collapsing them into a single narrative.

Data and discourse analysis

Uses structured data workflows to examine timelines, framing shifts, amplification patterns, and community-level dynamics.

Public research design

Turns research outputs into privacy-safe reports, visual summaries, timelines, and eventually more interactive tools.

Selected findings: June 22-30 reconstruction

The public version is anonymized and aggregated. Individual fan usernames, IDs, post IDs, direct links to fan posts, and re-identifying quotations are intentionally excluded.

Opposition was not one unified bloc

The reconstructed material is more consistent with multiple pre-existing grievances temporarily converging than with one stable group acting for one reason.

Existing-character fandom and Valko support could coexist

The source set includes counterexamples showing that support for an existing character did not automatically imply opposition to Valko.

A-0731 appears to have spread through distributed roles

Discovery, reframing, relay, amplification, and external mobilization were not necessarily performed by the same people.

The temporary coalition fragmented after cancellation

After the cancellation announcement, the overlapping opposition space rapidly split into different strategic and interpretive directions.

Event structure at a glance

June 22

Pre-existing grievances surface

Different fandoms frame the sixth-character issue through existing concerns around resources, story, monetization, identity, and character treatment.

June 23-28

Issue expansion

The dispute broadens into production capacity, operator trust, consumer agency, localization, and boycott strategy.

June 29

A-0731 escalation

The frame accelerates external-facing mobilization, but the recovered sequence suggests multiple roles rather than one identifiable originator.

June 30

Cancellation and fragmentation

The temporary overlap breaks apart into character-specific demands, continued boycott, cancellation criticism, and restoration calls.

Research principles

Privacy by default

Public outputs remove or generalize account-level identifiers wherever possible.

Findings are not interpretation

Directly supported findings are kept separate from later analytical inference.

Traceable methods

R, Python, Git, and structured evidence fields support reproducibility, revision history, and clearer audit trails.

This is an independent fan research project and is not affiliated with Infold Games or Papergames. The reconstructed dataset is not a census of Weibo and should not be interpreted as a population estimate of fandom opinion.
