# Valko Research Project

Independent research, fact-checking, and data analysis related to Valko / Ao Yin in *Love and Deepspace*.

This repository is the **public, privacy-preserving layer** of a broader research workflow. Public outputs are designed to preserve chronology, evidence structure, and analytical value while avoiding unnecessary exposure of individual fan accounts.

## Public research layers

The project separates three kinds of output:

① **Source reconstruction** - representative anonymized post-level entries showing date bands, community context, recurring tags / terms, paraphrased content, analytical role, and evidence notes.

② **Analysis** - findings derived from the reconstructed source set after separating direct evidence, poster interpretation, rumors, demands, and causal claims.

③ **Methods & ethics** - documentation of the collection route, platform-rule review, privacy safeguards, evidence-strength rules, and limits on future automated collection.

## Collection and ethics summary

For the June 22-30 reconstruction, candidate material was located through search-based public-web retrieval and then checked against publicly accessible Sina preservation pages, official Weibo pages, and related public traces.

This dataset was **not acquired through a researcher-operated high-frequency crawler making bulk requests directly to Weibo**. Weibo's Community Convention Article 54 restricts unauthorized automated or abnormally high-frequency behavior, including data extraction. This project does not claim formal platform approval, legal certification of compliance, or a general right to scrape Weibo.

Public outputs use data minimization and harm-reduction principles:

- fan usernames, user IDs, and post IDs are removed
- direct fan-post links are removed
- fan quotations are paraphrased rather than reproduced verbatim
- some timestamps are generalized to reduce re-identification risk
- account-level audit records remain outside the public layer
- reconstructed counts are not treated as estimates of fandom-wide prevalence

See the full methodology page for the Weibo rule review, PRC Personal Information Protection Law Article 27 considerations, AoIR Internet Research Ethics guidance, and the review standard for any future direct automated collection.

## Research principles

- privacy by default
- minimum necessary public detail
- clear separation between verified findings and interpretation
- traceable source provenance
- transparent uncertainty and revision history
- explicit distinction between posting a claim and verifying that claim
- no majority / prevalence claims from a non-representative reconstruction
