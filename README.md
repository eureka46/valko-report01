# Valko Research Project

A public, privacy-conscious reconstruction of the Valko / Ao Yin controversy in *Love and Deepspace*, with a focus on what was actually posted, when different frames appeared, and how the discussion changed over time.

## Research structure

The public project follows a simple research logic:

① **Background** - what happened around Valko / Ao Yin and why the June 22-30 period matters.

② **Methods** - which platform was examined, how posts were discovered and re-checked, how records were coded, and what the dataset can and cannot show.

③ **Results** - anonymized summaries of individual Weibo posts, observed hashtags / supertopics, and minute-level chronology where the sequence matters.

④ **Analysis & discussion** - what patterns are supported by the reconstructed records, with interpretation kept separate from directly observed material.

⑤ **Ethics & privacy** - how account-level evidence is protected and how residual re-identification risk is handled.

## Platform and dataset

The core June dataset is a reconstruction of **Weibo** discourse. Sina preservation pages and other public-web traces were used as supporting retrieval and verification surfaces; they are not treated as separate fandom platforms in the analysis.

The working reconstruction contains **217 logical records**. In the final audit, **202 URLs** were directly re-checked through preserved Sina pages; the remaining **15 records** required separate review through Weibo material, related-post views, profile-history evidence, image/source recovery, or other public traces.

This is a reconstructed corpus, not a census or probability sample. Record counts are not used to estimate fandom-wide prevalence or majority opinion.

## Public-data design

Public post-level records retain the chronology needed to understand the event, including observed date/time and relevant public hashtags where analytically necessary. To reduce exposure of individual fans, the public layer removes:

- fan usernames and handles
- user IDs and post IDs
- direct links to individual fan posts
- verbatim fan quotations
- unnecessary account-level attribution

Post content is rewritten as an individual-post paraphrase rather than reproduced as searchable source text. Because an exact timestamp plus a public hashtag can still leave some possibility of rediscovery, the public dataset should be understood as **de-identified / privacy-reduced**, not as a guarantee of irreversible anonymization.
